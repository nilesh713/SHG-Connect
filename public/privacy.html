SHG Connect must be opened through its Node.js server.
Do not open the HTML files directly or with Live Server. In the shg-connect folder run npm start, then open http://localhost:5000.

Privacy Policy

Prototype policy for the SHG Connect college field project.

What we collect

Name, email, mobile number, location and (for sellers) business details and an optional UPI ID. Order details include the delivery address you enter at checkout.

How it is used

Only to run the marketplace: showing products, processing orders, delivery updates and notifications. Customers see a seller's business name, location, description and (if the seller allows) a WhatsApp contact button. Sellers see the delivery name, address and phone for their own orders.

Passwords and payments

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and are never shown to anyone. SHG Connect does not collect or store card numbers, bank details or UPI PINs. UPI payments happen inside your own UPI app; only the transaction reference you type is saved.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time from the Profile page. To delete your account, contact the project team.
