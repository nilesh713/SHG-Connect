SHG Connect must be opened through its Node.js server.
Do not open the HTML files directly or with Live Server. In the shg-connect folder run npm start, then open http://localhost:5000.

Terms of Use

SHG Connect is a technology prototype built for a college field project.

Prototype notice

The platform demonstrates how digital tools can improve product visibility, customer reach, order management, delivery visibility, sales tracking, reviews and digital payment support for SHGs and artisans. It is not a commercial service.

Sellers

Sellers are responsible for accurate product information, fair prices, honest photos and delivering orders they confirm. Listings may be reviewed and removed by the admin.

Payments and delivery

Payments are made directly between customer and seller (UPI or Cash on Delivery). There is no payment gateway and no real-time GPS tracking; delivery status is updated by the seller.

Reviews

Only customers who received a product can review it. Abusive or misleading reviews may be hidden by the admin.
